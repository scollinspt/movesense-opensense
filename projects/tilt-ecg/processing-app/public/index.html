Methods and limitations

  • Beat-to-beat HR and R–R intervals use reviewed native ECG timestamps, not five-second aggregates.
  • Corrected angle uses reviewer-approved baseline, plateau, and achieved table-angle references.
  • HR and R–R regression use only the reviewer-defined ascending tilt interval.
  • Frequency-domain HRV is included only when its configured duration, NN quality, and stationarity requirements are met or explicitly overridden.
  • LF/HF is reported descriptively and is not labeled as a direct measure of sympathovagal balance.
